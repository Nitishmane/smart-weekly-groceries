Frequently asked questions

What does the app actually do?

It plans a week of meals at once and picks recipes that finish each other's packages — so the half bag of bean sprouts from Monday's pad thai gets used on Wednesday instead of going in the bin. Each weekly plan is scored by how little of what you buy spoils.

How does the free version differ from Plus?

Free gives you one generated plan per week with its full shopping list. Plus unlocks browsing all candidate plans, unlimited regeneration, pantry tracking, full nutrition breakdowns, and unlimited history.

How do I start the free trial?

The annual plan includes a 7-day free trial. Start it from any locked feature or from Settings. You can cancel during the trial and pay nothing.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Smart Weekly Groceries → Cancel. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

I paid but Plus features are locked.

Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore purchases. If that doesn't fix it, email us with the Apple receipt.

Is my data synced anywhere?

No. Everything lives on your device — see the privacy policy. That also means deleting the app deletes your plans and history.

Why does my week have fewer meals than I asked for?

Very narrow filters (say, vegan plus a single cuisine) can leave only a handful of eligible recipes. The app shows an honestly short week rather than padding it with repeats.
