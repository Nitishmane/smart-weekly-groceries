Privacy Policy

Effective September 9, 2026

The short version: Smart Weekly Groceries has no accounts and no tracking. Your meal plans, preferences, pantry, and history live only on your device. The only data that leaves your phone relates to in-app purchases (processed by Apple and RevenueCat) and, in the free version, the optional ads you choose to watch (served by Google AdMob, non-personalized).

Data we do not collect

The app does not collect, transmit, or store on any server: your food preferences, dietary restrictions, allergies, budget, meal plans, shopping lists, purchase history within the app, pantry contents, name, email address, location, contacts, or photos. There are no analytics SDKs and no tracking: the app never requests the advertising identifier (IDFA) and never asks to track you across other apps or websites.

Data stored on your device

Everything the app needs — your preferences, generated meal plans, shopping history, and pantry stock — is stored in a local database on your device. It is never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes this data permanently.

In-app purchases

Subscriptions are sold through Apple's App Store and managed by Apple. We never see your payment details. To validate purchases and remember your subscription status, the app uses RevenueCat, which processes:

  • A randomly generated, anonymous app user ID (not linked to your identity)
  • Your purchase history for this app (product, price, date, subscription status)
  • Basic device information (device model, OS version, app version, locale)

RevenueCat processes this data on our behalf to operate subscriptions and does not sell it. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Advertising (free version only)

The free version can show a rewarded ad — a video you explicitly choose to watch in exchange for an extra weekly plan generation. Ads never appear on their own: no banners, no pop-ups, and nothing plays unless you tap the button. Subscribers see no ads at all.

Ads are served by Google AdMob. We request non-personalized ads only: ads are selected from context, not from a profile of you, and the app does not use the advertising identifier. To serve and measure ads, Google receives basic technical data such as your IP address and device information, handled under the Google Privacy Policy. Aggregate ad revenue amounts (not your identity) are reported to RevenueCat alongside purchase data.

Managing and deleting your data

  • On-device data: delete the app to erase it.
  • Subscription: manage or cancel in your Apple ID settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions).
  • Purchase records held by RevenueCat: contact us (below) and we will request deletion of the anonymous purchase record.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the effective date revised before the change takes effect.
